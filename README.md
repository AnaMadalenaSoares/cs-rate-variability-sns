## About the study

This repository accompanies a longitudinal, observational study of caesarean section (CS) rate variability across 37 public hospitals of the Portuguese National Health Service (SNS), analysed at the hospital-year level over the period 2013–...... The analysis follows a logic of successive exclusion: it first establishes how much of the variability in CS rates cannot be explained by known structural factors (hospital level, management sector and region), and then examines what may account for the residual variation. A longitudinal panel model with unit and year fixed effects complements the descriptive analysis.

A distinctive feature of the study is the use of a large language model (Claude, by Anthropic) to support data analysis and the extraction of results through structured prompts. The prompts used are made available here so that the analytical process can be inspected and reproduced.

## Hospital codes

Hospitals are identified by codes A01–A37. These codes are not used for anonymisation but to simplify the analysis; the correspondence between each code and the name of the unit is provided in ..... (and in the dissertation itself). Codes A38–A40 appear in the source data but record no births and are not part of the study.

## Data sources

All data are aggregated at the hospital level and come from publicly available sources:

- **SNS Transparência** – births and caesarean sections by hospital ("partos-e-cesarianas")
- **ACSS** – Administração Central do Sistema de Saúde
- **ERS** – Entidade Reguladora da Saúde
- **INE** – Instituto Nacional de Estatística
- **Diário da República** – legal sources for the territorial reorganisation of health units (e.g. Decreto-Lei n.º 102/2023) and the 2026 Rede de Referenciação Hospitalar de Obstetrícia e Ginecologia
- .....

No individual patient data are included in this repository.
